**INDICATION:** 37-year-old male involved in an electric scooter crash. Positive FAST exam for free fluid and initial slight hemodynamic instability. Evaluate for traumatic lesions.

**TECHNIQUE:** Multidetector CT of the abdomen and pelvis was performed. Intravenous contrast was administered for arterial and portal venous phases. Coronal and sagittal reformations were reviewed.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver demonstrates a deep parenchymal laceration involving segments VI and VII, extending approximately 4 cm into the hepatic parenchyma. There is evidence of active extravasation of intravenous contrast within the laceration site, consistent with active hemorrhage. A moderate amount of high-attenuation free fluid is noted within the perihepatic space and the paracolic gutters, extending down into the pelvis, consistent with hemoperitoneum.

The gallbladder is unremarkable without wall thickening. The spleen, pancreas, and adrenal glands are normal in size and attenuation without evidence of injury. Both kidneys demonstrate symmetric enhancement and excretion; no perinephric hematoma is seen.

The gastrointestinal tract is unremarkable. There is no evidence of pneumoperitoneum. The aorta and its major branches are patent and maintain normal caliber.

Regarding incidental findings, there is a small, well-circumscribed, low-attenuation lesion in the left hepatic lobe measuring 1.2 cm, appearing characteristic of a simple cyst. Additionally, there is a mild prominence of the sigmoid colon without inflammatory changes. The urinary bladder is partially filled and appears intact.

The visualized portions of the lower thoracic cage show no acute fractures. The pelvic ring remains intact.

**CONCLUSION:**
Traumatic liver injury involving segments VI and VII with associated active bleeding and moderate hemoperitoneum.
