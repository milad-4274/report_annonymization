**INDICATION:** 22-year-old female presenting after an electric bike crash. Positive free fluid on FAST exam. Currently hemodynamically stable. Evaluate for traumatic lesions.

**TECHNIQUE:** Multidetector CT of the abdomen and pelvis was performed with intravenous contrast administration. Coronal and sagittal reformations were reviewed.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver demonstrates normal size and attenuation without evidence of laceration, hematoma, or contour abnormality. The gallbladder is unremarkable and no biliary ductal dilatation is noted.

Regarding the spleen, there is a small subcapsular hematoma measuring approximately 1.5 cm in maximum thickness along the superior pole. There is no deep parenchymal laceration or active extravasation of contrast medium. This is consistent with an AAST grade 1 splenic injury.

The pancreas is normal in morphology and enhancement. Both adrenal glands are unremarkable. The kidneys show symmetric enhancement and excretion without hydronephrosis or perinephric hematoma.

The gastrointestinal tract is unremarkable; there is no evidence of bowel wall thickening or pneumoperitoneum. A small amount of free fluid is noted in the pelvis and perihepatic space, likely representing a small volume of hemoperitoneum associated with the splenic injury.

The urinary bladder is midline and unremarkable. The uterus and ovaries are normal for the patient's age, although there is a small, simple 1 cm cyst in the left ovary, which is a common incidental finding.

The aorta and its major branches are patent and of normal caliber. No lymphadenopathy is identified. The visualized portions of the lower lungs are clear. The abdominal wall shows mild subcutaneous edema over the left flank, but no focal collection. The visualized osseous structures are intact without acute fracture.

**CONCLUSION:**
AAST grade 1 splenic injury characterized by a small subcapsular hematoma. Small volume of associated hemoperitoneum. No evidence of active hemorrhage.
