**CT CHEST AND ABDOMEN/PELVIS WITH CONTRAST**

**Indication:** 67-year-old female with history of clear cell renal cell carcinoma of the left kidney. Status post left nephrectomy 18 months ago with R1 resection margins. Evaluation for oncological recurrence.

**Technique:** Helical CT imaging was performed from the thoracic apex through the symphysis pubis following the administration of intravenous contrast. Axial images were reconstructed. Coronal and sagittal reformats were reviewed.

**Findings:**
The lungs are clear without evidence of suspicious pulmonary nodules, consolidation, or pleural effusion. There is no lymphadenopathy identified within the hila or mediastinum. The heart size is within normal limits, although there is mild atherosclerotic calcification noted along the wall of the ascending aorta. A small, stable hiatal hernia is present.

In the abdomen, the right kidney demonstrates normal enhancement and excretion without mass or hydronephrosis. The left kidney has been surgically removed; however, in the left retroperitoneum, specifically within the surgical bed adjacent to the psoas muscle, there is a poorly defined nodular soft tissue lesion measuring approximately 1.4 x 1.2 cm. This lesion demonstrates moderate contrast enhancement. There is no evidence of enlarged lymph nodes in the para-aortic or paracaval stations.

The liver is normal in size and contour with homogeneous attenuation and no focal lesions. The gallbladder is unremarkable. The spleen and pancreas are normal in appearance. The adrenal glands are bilateral and symmetric. The stomach and bowel loops are non-dilated and show no wall thickening. There is a small amount of benign-appearing cystic change in the lower pole of the right kidney, consistent with a simple cyst. The urinary bladder is well-distended with smooth walls. No free fluid or free air is seen within the peritoneal cavity. The visualized pelvic organs are unremarkable for the patient's age. The bony structures demonstrate mild degenerative changes of the lumbar spine.

**Conclusion:**
Appearance of a nodular enhancing lesion in the left retroperitoneal surgical bed, concerning for local oncological recurrence.
