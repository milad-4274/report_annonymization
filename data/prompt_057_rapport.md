**INDICATION:** 72-year-old male with Child-Pugh Class B cirrhosis. Screening for hepatocellular carcinoma.

**TECHNIQUE:** Multiplanar, multisequence imaging of the abdomen was performed before and after the administration of intravenous gadoxetate disodium. Dynamic post-contrast series were obtained during the arterial, portal venous, and delayed phases. Diffusion-weighted imaging was performed.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver demonstrates a nodular surface contour and heterogeneous parenchymal signal intensity consistent with the known history of cirrhosis. There is mild splenomegaly noted. The portal vein remains patent without evidence of thrombus, although there is a small amount of periportal collaterals. The gallbladder is present and unremarkable, showing no gallstones or wall thickening. The common bile duct is within normal limits for age.

Within segment 8 of the liver, there is a focal mass measuring 17 mm in maximum dimension. This lesion demonstrates intense non-rim arterial phase hyperenhancement with subsequent washout in the portal venous and delayed phases. It appears mildly hypointense on the hepatobiliary phase.

The pancreas is normal in morphology and signal intensity without focal masses or ductal dilatation. The adrenal glands are symmetric and unremarkable. Both kidneys show normal cortical thickness; however, there is a small, simple 6 mm cyst in the lower pole of the left kidney. No hydronephrosis is seen. The visualized portions of the bowel are non-dilated. There is a small volume of simple ascites present within the peritoneal cavity. No suspicious lymphadenopathy is identified in the retroperitoneum or porta hepatis.

**CONCLUSION:**
17 mm lesion in segment 8 of the liver meeting LI-RADS 5 criteria for hepatocellular carcinoma. No evidence of macrovascular invasion.
