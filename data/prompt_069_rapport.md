**INDICATION:** 75-year-old male presenting with diffuse abdominal pain. Laboratory results are reportedly within normal limits. Evaluate for colitis, mesenteric ischemia, or other intra-abdominal anomalies.

**TECHNIQUE:** Computed tomography of the abdomen and pelvis was performed. Intravenous contrast was administered for arterial and portal venous phase imaging. Oral contrast was provided for bowel opacification.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver demonstrates normal size and contour without focal mass lesions or intrahepatic biliary ductal dilatation. The gallbladder is present and unremarkable, showing no wall thickening or pericholecystic fluid. The spleen and pancreas are normal in appearance and morphology.

Regarding the gastrointestinal tract, there is no evidence of circumferential wall thickening or mucosal edema to suggest colitis. The mesenteric vessels are patent throughout their course, and there is no evidence of pneumatosis intestinalis or portal venous gas to indicate mesenteric ischemia. The appendix is visualized and appears normal.

The right kidney is normal in size and enhancement without hydronephrosis or calculi. In the left kidney, there is a well-defined cystic lesion measuring approximately 2.4 cm located in the lower pole. This cyst exhibits thin, slightly enhancing walls and a few fine internal septations, consistent with a Bosniak category II cyst. There is no associated perinephric stranding.

The urinary bladder is moderately distended with smooth walls. The pelvic organs are unremarkable for the patient's age.

There is a small amount of diverticulosis noted in the sigmoid colon without evidence of acute diverticulitis. Mild degenerative changes are seen in the lumbar spine, specifically at L4-L5, characterized by marginal osteophyte formation. No free intraperitoneal air or significant ascites is identified. The visualized lung bases are clear.

**CONCLUSION:**
Bosniak II cyst of the left kidney without associated complications.
