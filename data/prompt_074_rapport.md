**INDICATION:** 67-year-old female with history of clear cell carcinoma of the left kidney, status post left nephrectomy six months ago. Evaluation for oncological recurrence.

**TECHNIQUE:** Computed tomography was performed through the chest, abdomen, and pelvis. Axial images were acquired without intravenous contrast. Multiplanar reformats were reviewed.

**FINDINGS:**
The lungs are clear without evidence of suspicious pulmonary nodules or masses. There is no pleural effusion or pneumothorax. The mediastinal and hilar contours are within normal limits for the patient's age, and there is no significant lymphadenopathy identified in the axillary or mediastinal stations. The heart size is normal, though there is mild atherosclerotic calcification noted along the wall of the thoracic aorta.

In the abdomen, the right kidney is normal in size and position with no hydronephrosis or mass lesions. The left kidney has been surgically removed. In the left renal fossa, there is a well-circumscribed, fluid-attenuation collection measuring approximately 4 x 3 cm. This collection demonstrates thin walls and contains no internal enhancing components or solid nodules, consistent with a postoperative seroma. There is no evidence of an organized abscess or hematoma.

The liver is homogeneous in attenuation without focal lesions. The gallbladder is unremarkable. The spleen and pancreas demonstrate normal morphology and enhancement patterns. The adrenal glands are unremarkable. No free fluid is seen in the peritoneal cavity, and there is no evidence of peritoneal carcinomatosis.

The gastrointestinal tract is non-dilated. The urinary bladder is partially filled and appears smooth-walled. Within the pelvic region, there are small, benign-appearing uterine leiomyomas, which are common for this age group. No enlarged pelvic or retroperitoneal lymph nodes are visualized. The visualized osseous structures show mild degenerative changes of the lumbar spine and osteopenia, which is expected given the patient's demographics.

**CONCLUSION:**
Post-operative simple seroma in the left renal fossa; no evidence of oncological recurrence or associated complications.
