**INDICATION:** 72-year-old male with Child-Pugh Class B cirrhosis. Screening for hepatocellular carcinoma.

**TECHNIQUE:** Multiplanar, multisequence imaging of the abdomen was performed before and after the administration of intravenous gadoxetate disodium. Dynamic post-contrast series were obtained during the arterial, portal venous, and delayed phases.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver demonstrates a nodular surface contour and heterogeneous parenchymal signal intensity consistent with the known history of cirrhosis. There is mild splenomegaly noted. The portal vein is patent with normal hepatopetal flow, although there is a small amount of periportal edema. A focal lesion is identified within segment 8 measuring approximately 17 mm in maximum dimension. This lesion shows mild arterial phase hyperenhancement but lacks definitive washout on the portal venous or delayed phases; it appears slightly hypointense on the hepatobiliary phase.

The gallbladder is present without evidence of cholelithiasis or wall thickening. The common bile duct is within normal limits for the patient's age. The pancreas is unremarkable in morphology and signal intensity, showing some mild fatty infiltration. Both kidneys show normal cortical thickness and no suspicious masses, though there are a few small, simple cortical cysts bilaterally. The adrenal glands are symmetric and unremarkable.

There is a small volume of simple ascites noted within the perihepatic space. No lymphadenopathy is identified in the porta hepatis or retroperitoneum. The visualized portions of the bowel are non-dilated and show no wall thickening. The abdominal aorta is mildly ectatic but without aneurysm.

**CONCLUSION:**
A 17 mm lesion in segment 8 of the liver, categorized as LI-RADS 3. No evidence of portal vein thrombosis.
