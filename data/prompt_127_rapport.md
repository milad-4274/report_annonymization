**EXAMINATION:** MRI Abdomen (Liver Protocol)

**INDICATION:** 62-year-old male with history of pancreatic adenocarcinoma of the head, status post surgical resection three years ago. Presents with worsening cholestasis. Evaluate for liver metastases, disease recurrence, or biliary stenosis.

**TECHNIQUE:** Multiplanar, multisequence imaging was performed through the abdomen. T1 and T2 weighted sequences were obtained along with diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI). Dynamic contrast-enhanced imaging was performed during the arterial, portal venous, and delayed phases. MRCP sequences were utilized for biliary tree evaluation.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver demonstrates normal size and contour. There is no focal mass lesion identified within the hepatic parenchyma to suggest metastasis or recurrence. Signal intensity is homogeneous across all segments. The portal vein and hepatic veins are patent without evidence of thrombosis. A small, simple cyst is noted in segment IV, measuring approximately 8 mm, which is an incidental finding.

Regarding the biliary system, there is diffuse dilatation of the intra-hepatic bile ducts throughout both lobes of the liver. This dilatation extends down to the level of the previous biliary anastomosis, where there is abrupt narrowing. The extra-hepatic biliary tree distal to the anastomosis appears normal in caliber. No choledocholithiasis is visualized. The gallbladder is surgically absent.

The pancreas shows postoperative changes at the head and neck. The remaining pancreatic parenchyma demonstrates normal signal intensity and morphology without suspicious masses or focal enlargement. The pancreatic duct is not significantly dilated.

The spleen, adrenals, and kidneys are unremarkable. No suspicious lymphadenopathy is seen in the porta hepatis, peripancreatic region, or retroperitoneum. There is a small amount of free fluid in the perihepatic space, likely physiologic. No ascites is present. The visualized bowel loops are non-dilated and show no wall thickening.

**CONCLUSION:**
Diffuse intra-hepatic bile duct dilatation proximal to the biliary anastomosis, consistent with an anastomotic stricture. No evidence of biliary obstruction due to recurrent malignancy or liver metastases.
