**INDICATION:** 54-year-old female presenting with right flank pain for 24 hours. Evaluate for urinary stones or cholecystitis.

**TECHNIQUE:** Non-contrast helical CT of the abdomen and pelvis was performed. Axial images were obtained and reconstructed in coronal and sagittal planes.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver is normal in size and contour. There is no evidence of gallbladder wall thickening or pericholecystic fluid; the gallbladder appears unremarkable without radiopaque calculi. The spleen, pancreas, and adrenal glands are within normal limits for age.

Regarding the gastrointestinal tract, there is no bowel wall thickening or obstructive pattern identified. A small amount of diverticulosis is noted in the sigmoid colon without evidence of acute diverticulitis.

The kidneys are normal in size and position. On the right side, there is no evidence of nephrolithiasis or hydronephrosis. On the left side, there is a small, 3 mm calcified density located within the proximal ureter. Despite this, there is no associated upstream dilatation of the left renal collecting system or ureter. The urinary bladder is partially filled and shows smooth wall thickness.

The pelvic organs are unremarkable for the patient's age. There are a few small, benign-appearing uterine leiomyomas. No free fluid or free air is seen within the peritoneal cavity. The visualized lung bases are clear. The abdominal aorta is normal in caliber without aneurysmal dilation. Visualized osseous structures show mild degenerative changes of the lumbar spine.

**CONCLUSION:**
Left proximal ureteral 3 mm stone without associated upstream dilatation.
