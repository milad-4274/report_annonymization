**INDICATION:** 76-year-old male with known uncompensated cirrhosis. Evaluate liver morphology, assess for portal vein thrombosis, and quantify amount of ascites.

**TECHNIQUE:** Axial CT imaging was performed through the abdomen and pelvis (AP) following the administration of intravenous contrast. Multiplanar reformats were reviewed.

**FINDINGS:**
The liver demonstrates a dysmorphic contour with surface nodularity and hypertrophy of the caudate lobe relative to the right lobe, which appears somewhat atrophied. There is a general heterogeneity to the hepatic parenchyma. The portal vein remains patent throughout its course; no evidence of thrombus or filling defects is identified. The splenic vein and superior mesenteric vein are patent. The spleen is enlarged, measuring approximately 14 cm in craniocaudal dimension. There is a moderate volume of free fluid within the peritoneal cavity, noted primarily in the perihepatic space and the pelvic cul-de-sac.

The gallbladder is present without wall thickening or pericholecystic fluid. The common bile duct is within normal limits for age. The pancreas is unremarkable in morphology and attenuation, showing some mild fatty infiltration. Both kidneys demonstrate normal enhancement and excretion without hydronephrosis or suspicious masses.

Within the pelvis, the urinary bladder is partially filled and smooth-walled. There is a small, benign-appearing prostatic enlargement consistent with the patient's age. A few small, non-obstructing renal cortical cysts are noted in the left kidney. The visualized bowel loops are non-dilated and show no wall thickening. No significant lymphadenopathy is seen in the retroperitoneum or pelvic regions. The visualized osseous structures show mild degenerative changes of the lumbar spine.

**CONCLUSION:**
Dysmorphic liver with radiological features of compensated portal hypertension. No evidence of portal vein thrombosis. Moderate ascites is present.
