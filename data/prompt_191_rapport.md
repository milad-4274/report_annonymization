**INDICATION:** 79-year-old female presenting with low velocity head trauma. Patient is currently on anticoagulation therapy. Evaluate for intracranial hemorrhage and acute fractures.

**TECHNIQUE:** Non-contrast axial CT imaging of the head was performed. Multiplanar reformats were reviewed.

**FINDINGS:**
The brain parenchyma demonstrates expected age-related volume loss, characterized by mild prominence of the cortical sulci and ventricular system. There is no evidence of acute intracranial hemorrhage, midline shift, or mass effect. No territorial infarcts or suspicious intra-axial collections are identified.

The basal cisterns remain open and patent. The ventricular morphology is symmetric and unremarkable for the patient's age.

Evaluation of the skull base and calvarium reveals a non-displaced fracture involving the left temporal bone. This fracture line does not extend into the petrous portion of the temporal bone; the inner ear structures and the carotid canal appear intact. No other cranial vault fractures are seen.

The paranasal sinuses and mastoid air cells are clear. There is some mild mucosal thickening in the maxillary sinuses, likely chronic. The visualized portions of the orbits and the globe appearances are within normal limits.

**CONCLUSION:**
Left temporal bone fracture without involvement of the petrous part. No associated intracranial hemorrhage.
