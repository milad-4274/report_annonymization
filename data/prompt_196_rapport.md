**INDICATION:** 71-year-old male who suffered a fall from a ladder landing on his buttocks. Initial pelvic radiographs were reported as negative for fracture. Evaluate for occult pelvic fracture.

**TECHNIQUE:** Helical CT imaging of the pelvis was performed without intravenous contrast. Multiplanar reformats were reviewed.

**FINDINGS:**
The pelvic ring is intact overall, however, there are non-displaced fractures involving the left ischio-pubic and ilio-pubic rami. There is no significant displacement of the fracture fragments and no extension into the obturator foramen. The sacroiliac joints are symmetric and well-aligned without evidence of widening or dislocation. The pubic symphysis is maintained within normal limits. Both hip joints demonstrate mild marginal osteophyte formation and narrowing of the joint spaces, consistent with age-related degenerative changes.

The bladder is partially filled and appears unremarkable without wall thickening. The prostate is mildly enlarged for the patient's age but otherwise shows a normal contour. There is no free fluid or hematoma identified within the pelvic cavity or the soft tissues surrounding the pelvic girdle. The visualized portions of the bowel are non-dilated and show no obvious wall thickening. The visualized lower thoracic spine demonstrates mild multilevel spondylosis.

**CONCLUSION:**
Non-displaced fractures of the left ischio-pubic and ilio-pubic rami. No associated pelvic hematoma or instability.
